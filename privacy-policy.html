Privacy Policy

Crystal Miner by ShardWorks Interactive · Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Crystal Miner (the "game", Android package com.shardworks.crystalminer) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The game is made by ShardWorks Interactive, an independent developer in Western Australia ("we", "us").

In short: you can play the whole game without an account, and then your progress stays on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store your game save in the cloud so you can restore it. You can delete your account and cloud save at any time.

Information we collect

Game progress on your device. Your progress and settings are saved on your phone. This stays on your device unless you sign in.

Your Google account details (only if you sign in). Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from your Google account through Google Firebase Authentication. We use these only to identify your cloud save.

Your cloud save (only if you sign in). A copy of your game progress (for example gold, mines, upgrades and settings) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, on servers in Sydney, Australia. It is updated every few minutes while you play and when you leave the game.

Purchases. Any in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.

Ads. The game may offer optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads, under Google's own privacy policy.

We do not collect your phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data, and we do not use analytics or tracking of our own.

How we use information

  • To save, back up and restore your game progress across devices.
  • To provide in-app purchases.
  • To show optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to running the game.

Who we share it with

We do not share your information with anyone except the service providers that run parts of the game for us, all operated by Google:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and cloud saves
  • Google Play: app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Google AdMob: rewarded ads

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Information is sent over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your cloud save and account details are kept for as long as your account exists. Progress on your phone stays until you reset the game in Settings, clear the app's data or uninstall it.

To delete your account and cloud save: in the game, open More › Settings › Cloud Save and tap Delete cloud save & account. Or follow the steps on our account deletion page, including requesting deletion by email. Deleted data is not kept.

You can also sign out at any time in the same Settings screen. Signing out stops cloud saving but does not delete your cloud save.

Children

Crystal Miner is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. If you are in Australia and are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
