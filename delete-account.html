Delete your Crystal Miner account and data

Crystal Miner by ShardWorks Interactive

Signing in with Google is optional. It creates a Crystal Miner account so your progress can be backed up to the cloud. You can delete that account and its data at any time.

Option 1: delete in the game (instant)

  1. Open Crystal Miner and tap More, then Settings.
  2. Under Cloud Save, tap Delete cloud save & account, then tap again to confirm.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the game installed, email shardworksgames@gmail.com with the subject Crystal Miner account deletion. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, so we can match it to your account. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Crystal Miner account record: your Google account's name, email address and account ID.
  • Your cloud save (game progress stored on our servers).

Nothing is kept after deletion. Progress saved on your own phone is not affected; it stays on the device until you uninstall the game or reset it in Settings.
